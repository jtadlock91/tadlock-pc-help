Hi, I'm John.

I live right here in Kearny with my family, and I've been building, upgrading, and reviving PCs for years — my own, my kids', and half the machines my friends and family own. I'm a dad first, so I know exactly what's really on these computers: the school projects, the family photos, the stuff you can't replace. Keeping all of that safe and running is something I already do every day at home — doing it for my neighbors just comes naturally.

I started Tadlock PC Help because folks out here shouldn't have to drive to the Valley and pay city prices for honest computer work. When I'm not fixing computers, I'm running the home server that stores our family's photos and movies — it's also serving the page you're reading right now. I work a regular job during the week, so most repairs happen evenings and weekends — which also means I treat every machine like my reputation depends on it, because in a town this size, it does.
